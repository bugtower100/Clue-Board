I. Summary of Circumstances

I. 情况摘要

The decedent's body was discovered in a drainage ditch near 25 Blue Island Avenue on the morning of December 22, 2009, at approximately 06:20. The body was reportedly found by a milk delivery person and recovered from the scene by Chicago Police Department (CPD) officers.

死者遗体于在蓝岛大街25号附近的排水沟内被发现，时间是2009年12月22日早晨06:20。据称，遗体由送奶工发现，并由芝加哥警察局（CPD）警员在现场提取。

II. Condition of the Body

II. 尸体状况

Preliminary examination revealed that the body was in an advanced state of decomposition, with multiple postmortem injuries and signs of environmental exposure. Specifically:

初步检查发现，尸体已处于高度腐烂状态，并伴有多处死后创伤及环境暴露迹象。具体包括：

  • The body sustained severe damage, with significant loss of soft tissue.
  • The tongue was missing, possibly removed before or after death by an unknown means.
  • Evidence of rodent activity, including typical gnaw marks on exposed limbs and facial structures.
  • The deceased exhibited severe blood loss, with significant internal and external bleeding evident.
  • The body was found in a state of static water immersion, with an estimated immersion time of several hours. Skin sloughing and waterlogged softening consistent with prolonged immersion.
  • 尸体遭受严重毁损，大量软组织缺失。
  • 舌头缺失，可能死前或死后以不明方式被移除。
  • 存在啮齿类动物活动迹象，包括暴露的四肢和面部结构上的典型啮咬痕迹。
  • 死者出现严重失血，大量内外出血表现明显。
  • 尸体被发现时处于静水浸泡状态，浸泡时间估计数小时。皮肤滑脱和水浸软化现象与长时间浸泡一致。
III. Time of Death

III. 死亡时间

Due to the degree of decomposition and exposure to the aquatic environment, the postmortem interval cannot be precisely determined. The estimated time of death is consistent with approximately 8 to 24 hours prior to discovery, but remains uncertain.

由于尸体分解程度和水环境暴露，无法确切确定死后间隔时间。估计死亡时间与发现前约8至24小时一致，但仍不确定。

IV. Cause and Manner of Death (Preliminary)

IV. 死亡原因和方式（初步）

Manner of death: Homicide (suspected)

死亡原因：待定（待毒理学检测和进一步检查）
死亡方式：疑似他杀
