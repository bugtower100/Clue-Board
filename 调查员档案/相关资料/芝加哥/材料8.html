Landmark Building Finds Buyer

城市陆标找到买主

Chicago Tribune | Date: October 19, 2009 | Reporter: Leonard Weber

After years of inactivity, the Union Theatre, one of Chicago's oldest performing arts venues, has been acquired by Metropolis Investment, represented by Chicago Commercial Bank.

在经过多年沉寂之后，联合剧院，芝加哥历史最悠久的演艺馆之一，被芝加哥商行所代理的大都会投资收购。

The investment firm officially announced that plans for the future development of this massive historic building will be unveiled in the near future.

投资商行正式宣布，对于这个巨大的老建筑的后续开发计划将会在短期内公布。

Located on Grand Avenue, the Union Theatre originally opened in 1891 under the name Union Auditorium and closed in 2005, remaining vacant ever since.

位于格兰德大道的联合剧院最初以联合演艺馆的名字于1891年开张并于2005年关闭，此后一直闲置至今。
