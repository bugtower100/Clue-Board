Chicago Mercantile & Trade League Summer Procurement Fair Held at the Nick Boek Hotel

【芝加哥商贸联盟夏季采购博览会在尼克·博克酒店举行】

Business Representatives Gather at North Shore Landmark

【商业代表齐聚北岸地标】

Source: Chicago Tribune ｜Date of publication:July 20, 2009｜Reporter: Ben Hartman

On Monday, the Chicago Mercantile & Trade League successfully hosted its 2009 Summer Mid-Year Procurement Bidding Event at the North Shore landmark Nick's Hotel. The event attracted over 30 supplier representatives from Illinois, Wisconsin, and Michigan, covering multiple bidding categories including transportation, equipment, building materials, security systems, and light industrial services.

【周一，芝加哥商贸联盟在北岸地标尼克酒店成功举办了2009年夏季年中采购招标活动。该活动吸引了来自伊利诺伊州、威斯康星州和密歇根州的30多名供应商代表，涵盖运输、设备、建筑材料、安全系统和轻工业服务等多个招标类别。】

The event took place in the hotel's third-floor banquet hall and followed a "closed-door bidding" format, with some contracts limited to sealed bids from invited companies. A spokesperson for the Chicago Mercantile & Trade League stated that the bidding process focused on "repairing and improving earthquake-damaged areas to bring new life to the north and south."

【活动在酒店三楼宴会厅举行，采用"闭门招标"形式，部分合同仅限受邀公司密封投标。芝加哥商贸联盟发言人表示，招标过程重点关注"修复和改善地震受损地区，为南北地区带来新生机"。】

The Chicago Chamber of Commerce stated that the final list of winning companies will be announced in August, with some results "involving confidential city projects and therefore not publicly disclosed." As of the time of publication, the organizers had not responded to inquiries regarding whether the event involved municipal renovation contracts.

【芝加哥商会表示，最终中标公司名单将于8月公布，部分结果"涉及机密城市项目，因此不会公开披露"。截至发稿时，组织者尚未回应关于该活动是否涉及市政改造合同的询问。】
