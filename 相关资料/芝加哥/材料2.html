Homeless man brutally murdered

流浪汉遭到残杀

Source: Chicago Tribune ｜ Date of publication: February 15, 2003 ｜ Special correspondent: Ley Klab

On February 12, 2003, police discovered the body of an unidentified homeless person in an abandoned lot behind the United Theater on Grand Avenue in Chicago. The body was in a highly decomposed state, indicating it had been exposed to the elements for several days.

不明身份的流浪者的残缺尸体被警方于2月12日被发现，发现地点是格兰德大道联合剧院后面的闲置地上。根据警方所说，尸体发现时已腐烂较为严重，也就是说暴尸于此已有一定时日。

A police spokesperson stated that the victim's remains were largely dismembered, and the coroner preliminarily determined that the severe injuries on the body were likely caused by wild animals. Since the body was not carrying any identification documents, the victim's identity remains unconfirmed.

验尸官说尸体损伤严重，好像是野生动物所为。遇害者没有任何身份证明。

Currently, police speculate that the victim may have been a homeless individual seeking shelter from the cold winter weather when the tragic incident occurred. Police have increased patrols in the area and are urging witnesses to provide any relevant information to assist with the investigation.

警方推测遇害者可能是一个寻找避寒之处的流浪汉。警方已加强该地区巡逻，并敦促目击者提供相关信息协助调查。

Community residents expressed shock and concern over the incident, urging greater protection measures for the homeless population to prevent similar tragedies from occurring again. Social welfare organizations also stated that they will strive to increase assistance for the homeless, particularly during harsh winter weather, by providing them with safe shelter.

社区居民对此事件表示震惊和担忧，敦促加强对流浪汉群体的保护措施。社会福利组织也表示将努力增加对流浪汉的援助，特别是在严寒天气期间为他们提供安全庇护所。

The case is currently under further investigation, and the police have pledged to clarify the truth as soon as possible and ensure justice for the victim.

案件目前正在进一步调查中，警方承诺将尽快查明真相，为受害者伸张正义。
