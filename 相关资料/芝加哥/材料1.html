A brutal gangland murder
The motive remains a mystery.
Source: Chicago Tribune ｜ Date of publication: December 19, 2009 ｜ Correspondent:Roy Rizzo

The mutilated body of gangster Bruno Lanzetti was found in a drainage ditch yesterday morning.

黑帮歹徒布鲁诺·兰泽蒂残缺不全的尸体于昨日早晨在排水渠中被发现。

A milkman discovered Lanceretti's body on his usual delivery route before dawn. Lanceretti was 31 years old and a member of the notorious Marquette Park Gang.

一个送奶工在他往常送货的路上，也还是黎明之前时发现了兰泽蒂的尸体。兰泽蒂终年31岁，是臭名昭著的马凯特公园帮（Marquette Park Gang）的一名成员。

The police currently have no leads, but they have linked Lanceretti's murder to the escalation of violent clashes between rival gangs following the indictment of notorious crime boss Al Capone earlier this year.

警方现在还毫无线索，但是他们将兰泽蒂的被害与今年初著名的犯罪头目艾尔·卡彭被指控之后，敌对帮派之间的暴力斗争升级联系在了一起。

According to Raffi McTighe, spokesperson for the Cooks Town Sheriff's Office, more lives will be lost before this gang feud is settled.

根据库克镇警长办公室的发言人拉菲·麦克提格所说，在这场黑帮恩怨平息之前，还会有更多的生命被夺去。
