A brutal gangland murder
Private detective killed in Bridgeport explosion.

私人侦探在布里奇波特爆炸中遇害

Source: Chicago Tribune ｜ Date of publication: December 30, 2009 ｜ Correspondent:Frank Cluff

A private detective was killed late Saturday night in an explosion at a small office in the Irish district of Bridgeport.

一名私人侦探于周六深夜在布里奇波特的爱尔兰区主区的一间小型办公室内发生爆炸后遇害。

The incident occurred shortly after midnight at the intersection of Holstede and 35th Street. The victim has been identified as Jack Sullivan, who was in his office at the time of the explosion.

该事件发生于午夜过后不久，地点位于霍尔斯特德和35号大街的交汇处。已经确认遇害者为杰克·沙利文，爆炸时他本人正好处于办公室中。

According to the autopsy, he died from smoke inhalation caused by the fire that followed the explosion.

据尸检表明，他因爆炸引发的火灾导致吸入浓烟而死亡。

Police stated that the explosion appears to have been intentional, with preliminary reports indicating the possible use of an improvised explosive device. No arrests have been made, and no suspects have been identified at this time.

警方表示，此次爆炸似乎是蓄意行为，初步报告显示可能使用了简易爆炸装置。目前尚未有人被捕，目前尚未锁定嫌疑人。

"This was a targeted attack," a police spokesperson said. "We are pursuing all leads and urge anyone with information to come forward."

"这是一起有针对性的袭击，"一名警方发言人表示，"我们正在追查所有线索，并敦促任何掌握信息的人士挺身而出。"

The investigation is ongoing.

调查仍在进行中。
